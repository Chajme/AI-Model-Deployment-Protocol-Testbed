|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 20261123T123652_amqp_iot_r1          | 0.054 s | 87.319 s | 0.480 Mbit/s | True | 11.05 % |

- Delivery goodput stays below the 0.512 Mbit/s tbf rate: the device link is
  shaped. Before the fix the MQTT receiver logged up to 1724 Mbit/s (FIXED-15).
- Device-leg overhead is ~11 % instead of the ~115 % two-leg legacy figure.
- mqtt-windowed ~= mqtt: with the publisher on the clean link, the per-chunk
  ack wait no longer limits throughput; the device link does. The legacy MQTT
  plateau (~0.16 Mbit/s) was largely a product of the legacy shaping.

Problems found and fixed by these runs:
1. The harness used to stop the capture and the stack as soon as the SENDER
   exited. With an unshaped publisher that is ~0.06 s, long before the broker
   finished delivering to the device, so no receiver row was written. The
   harness now waits for the receiver's verified row (and fails fast if the
   receiver container stops).
2. RabbitMQ reset the receiver's connection mid-transfer: its default 15 s
   socket send_timeout is far shorter than writing a 1 MB message over a
   512 kbit/s link. protocols/AMQP/broker/rabbitmq.conf raises it to 600 s
   (heartbeat 600 s).
